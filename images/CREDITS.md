## Styles

The nine landscape style pictures (img/styles/*-800.webp and *-1600.webp) are AI-generated illustrations made with Grok Bot image generation (1024×576 originals, upscaled to 1600 px wide). They show the general look of each style, not a real garden or a specific design. They have no photographer credit. The earlier Wikimedia Commons style photos were removed in v14.

## 3D models, textures and libraries (removed in v12)
v12 removed all 3D: the three.js engine, Poly Haven models, textures and the HDRI are no longer shipped (archived outside the app in /workspace/yardplan-archive-3d). The site plan is a 2D SVG drawing made for Yardplan.
